# Job Search & Candidate Guidelines for Noor Ullah

Whenever the user asks to find, scout, or fetch jobs:

## 1. Candidate Background
- **Candidate:** Noor Ullah (Senior Frontend / Full-Stack Engineer, 4+ years experience).
- **Core Stack:** React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, Zustand, modern component systems, REST/GraphQL.
- **Key Highlight:** Architected and scaled TechBazaar.pk from 0 to 300K+ active users with 90+ Lighthouse performance scores.
- **Location:** Pakistan (UTC+5).

## 2. Job Search Rules & Filters
- **Batch Size:** Always provide **4 to 5 jobs** so each can be easily reviewed.
- **Platforms Supported:**
  - **LinkedIn Jobs** (Direct company postings, Remote, worldwide/open to Pakistan).
  - Direct ATS Boards: **Ashby**, **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, **Y Combinator (Work at a Startup)**, and **Wellfound**.
- **Location Check (Crucial):**
  - Fully remote and globally available (**Worldwide / Anywhere / Everywhere / Open to Pakistan**).
  - Explicitly check the Job Description for geographic restrictions. If it is restricted to US, Canada, UK, or EU residents/citizens only, **exclude it**.
- **Recency (Strict 2-3 Days Check):**
  - Maximum age of posting must be within the **latest 2 to 3 days (48 to 72 hours)** of the search date.
  - Actively verify exact `publishedAt` / `created_at` timestamps to filter out older evergreen listings and lingering reposts.
- **Authenticity (No Spam):**
  - Only real, authentic companies and startups.
  - Filter out repetitive scraper spam, ghost jobs, and hiring agencies (e.g. Crossover, BairesDev, Turing, DevsData). On LinkedIn, prioritize direct company listings over third-party recruiter reposts.
- **Format:**
  - Company & Title with direct application / LinkedIn link.
  - Exact publication date / age (e.g., "Published yesterday" or "Published 2 days ago").
  - Verified remote location status.
  - Tech stack match.
  - Why it fits Noor's profile.
  - Any special application instructions or attention tests (e.g. prompt keywords).
