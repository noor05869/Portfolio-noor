# Canvas Design Philosophy

Creates museum and magazine quality layouts using systematic visual precision, intentional limited color palettes, bold spatial hierarchy, and pristine execution.

## Essential Principles
1. **Geometric Silence & Structural Honesty**:
   - Layouts are structured by strong grid alignments and intentional rhythm.
   - Elimination of visual sludge, foggy overlays, and random decorations.
2. **Text as Precision Architecture**:
   - High contrast, pristine letterspacing (`tracking-tight` for titles, `tracking-widest` for monospace metadata).
   - Generous margins and breathing room: nothing crowds, nothing overlaps unintentionally.
3. **Master-Level Craftsmanship**:
   - Every surface looks labored over with care: precise border radius, harmonious color hierarchy, and intentional focal points.
   - High resolution media with tasteful framing, device-like or studio-grade presentation cards, and clean interaction layers.
